## Level 1 — Typography and mechanics

Defaults below are conventions; a style profile may override (except Epistemics in SKILL.md).

- **Em dashes:** avoid the spaced, formulaic kind. Restructure with comma, colon, parentheses, or two sentences. Hyphens stay in compounds. (Density tell: 1–2 in 300w often fine; five = signature; zero across a long piece can also be a signature.)
- **Quotation marks:** pick one style and hold it. Default for web: straight.
- **Headings:** sentence case — first word and proper nouns. Title Case On Every Word is an AI habit.
- **Bold:** not on every “key term”. Bold only where the reader would get lost without it.
- **Lists:** do not convert flowing prose into vertical lists with bolded inline headers and a colon in every item. Fewer than four items, or items that flow: keep as a paragraph.
- **Emoji** in headings/bullets: no, unless the genre demands it.
- **No “Conclusion” / “In summary” / “Overall” sections** that restate. Endings need something new: a decision, next step, or consequence. ↳ structural Thematic Explicitness / Mode of Resolution
- **Markdown / chatbot debris:** `---` before headings, fences around prose, `utm_source=chatgpt.com`, `turn0search0`, `oaicite`, placeholders like `[Your Name]`. Delete silently when rewriting.

## Safe vs overshoot-risky

- **Subtraction-safe:** delete costs nothing → absolute ban on the *category* is OK.
- **Overshoot-risky:** construction is normal English; only **rate** is diagnostic → frequency target, not hard zero.

## 15 surface rules (Pass S)

### Cut

| Tell | Class | Tier | Rule | Calibrated |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Em dashes | risky | T3 | Prefer commas/periods/parentheses; watch density | Existence ≠ tell; density is |
| Stock openers | safe | T3 | “Here's the thing,” “But here's the kicker,” “Let that sink in” | Absolute ban on category |
| The “most” hook | risky | T3 | Never open with manufactured “Most people…” — open with number, name, or scene | Quantifier OK when true. ↳ structural Intertextual named |
| False negatives | safe | T2 | Never “It's not about X, it's about Y” as strawman | Best-evidenced LinkedIn pattern; keep if X is a real belief |
| Anaphora stacks | risky | T3 | No stacked same-opening structures | One deliberate parallel OK |
| Staccato stacks | risky | T3 | No stacked short fragments; max one fragment stack per piece | Tell is the stack |
| Reversal framing | risky | T3 | No decorative mirror sentences | If plain version says the same, cut symmetry |
| Pseudo rhetorical Q | risky | T3 | “The reality?” “The result?” banned | Genuine unanswered questions stay |
| Metronome | risky | T3 | Vary sentence length on purpose | Diagnose by reading aloud |
| Visual formatting | safe | T3 | LinkedIn: no bold/italics/underline/emoji bullets | Platform-scoped only |

## Lexicon exemplars (fast path)

Full lists + category 19 clean slop: [blacklist.md](blacklist.md).

**Safe — stock openers / fake suspense:** here's the thing · let that sink in · the reality? · but here's the kicker · this resonates · at its core · in today's fast-paced world · dive deep · let's be honest · spoiler alert · a testament to · stark reminder

**Safe — corporate verbs / dead metaphors:** delve · leverage (v.) · unlock · unleash · elevate · empower · seamless · robust · transformative · game-changer · tapestry · journey/landscape/navigate/ecosystem (metaph.)

**Risky — decorative formality (rate):** crucial · pivotal · furthermore · moreover · in conclusion — delete the connective, keep the sentence; one crucial/pivotal per piece OK.

Near-misses and synonyms of the same kind count as the same hit. Zero listed-token hits is not success.

## 60-second scan

1. Four literal searches: em dash, `not about`, `here's the`, `The reality`
2. First line: number, name, or scene? Else template hook
3. Read aloud once: keynote → text-to-friend
4. Find the opinion: if no committee-cuttable sentence, nothing at stake
5. Ending: summarize/engagement → cut last two lines
6. Shape: same paragraph lengths → break one
7. Outline test + slack + aphorism budget (SKILL.md final check)
8. Grep blacklist markers if shell available

## Platform notes

- **LinkedIn:** no bold/italics/underline/emoji bullets; longer posts need Pass S more, not less
- **Email:** cut “I hope this email finds you well”; open with reason; one ask
- **Instagram captions:** tells apply; rhythm dominates; one usable thing without the video
- **Cold DMs:** if the first sentence could go to a hundred people unchanged, rewrite until it can only go to this one
