# Non-fiction mapping (unvalidated)

**WARNING — open every use with this:** Nothing in this table is measured. STORYSCOPE corpus ≈5,000-word literary fiction; subplot/anachrony have no direct counterpart in a changelog. Mapping is reasoned from **mechanism** → expository analogue — **hypothesis**, not evidence. Keep fiction rubric ([structural.md](structural.md)) and this layer separate. Under ~1,000 words this page does not apply (route to Pass S / [surface.md](surface.md)).

**Last reviewed:** 2026-09-29 · status: hypothesis

Promote from hypothesis to guidance only after a paired self-test (with/without constraints).

## Mapping table

| Narrative axis | Underlying mechanism | Non-fiction analogue | Conf | Dual-provenance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Thematic Explicitness & Moralizing | Over-determination — refuses inferred meaning | Delete closing restatement (“What this means…”, “The lesson…”, “Ultimately…”). If the argument worked, the reader has it | **high** | Surface endings — no summary, no motivational bow |
| Narratorial Thematic Commentary | Narrator steps outside to explain significance | Cut meta on own writing: “It's worth noting…”, “Importantly…”, “This is a crucial distinction because…” | **high** | Surface lexicon furthermore/moreover/in conclusion — same mechanism; **do not count both** |
| Intertextual Strategy → explicit named | Avoidance of verifiable specifics | Name study, person, version, date, product, dollar figure. Replace “research suggests” / “many teams find”. **Highest-value transfer** | **high** | Surface specificity + no fake war stories |
| Moral Polarity → ambivalent | Refusal to leave tension unresolved | State real cost of recommendation; don't immediately neutralize | **high** | — |
| Mode of Resolution → internal understanding | Tidy closure | Not every piece needs a conclusion. End on unresolved question / open case / thing you don't know | **high** | Surface endings, paired with Thematic Explicitness |
| Thematic Unity | Every element pressed into one point | One genuine digression that doesn't advance the thesis | medium | — |
| Subplot Integration → no subplots | Single-track argument | Genuine second thread: serious counter-argument or complicating parallel case | medium | — |
| Dominant Emotional Expression → embodied | Formulaic figurative substitution | Reflexive figure (“not just X but Y”, metaphor per paragraph). Sometimes state the thing | medium | — |
| Continuity of Main Causal Chain | Each point tidily produces next | Gaps, dead ends, unconnected facts; say when you don't know why something works | medium | — |
| Sensory Density & Spatial Granularity | Descriptive padding | Cut throat-clearing landscape / “increasingly important” openers; start at the first idea | medium | — |
| Fourth-Wall & Direct Reader Address | Text acknowledges reader | 2nd person already normal; acknowledge skips, thin spots, real objections | medium | — |
| Depth of Recontextualization After Surprise | Later info re-prices earlier | Later section changes how earlier reads (analysis / post-mortems) | medium | — |
| Chronological Discontinuity & Anachrony | Non-sequential presentation | **Weak.** Outcome-first post-mortems under-used; don't disrupt expository logic for its own sake | **low** | — |
| Location Variety Scope | Range of concrete settings | Loose: example variety across domains | **low** | — |
| Dialogue-to-Narration Proportion | Direct speech vs summary | Quote source/error/commit rather than paraphrase — related, not same mechanism | **low** | — |

## Confidence split

- **High confidence:** thematic over-determination, intertextual vagueness, tidy resolution — map almost directly.
- **Low confidence:** temporal complexity, location variety, dialogue proportion — weak analogues; most likely to produce bad advice. Mark speculative if used.

## Dual-provenance (state without hedging; still not “measured”)

Independently corroborated by the surface layer (platform detection + editing practice):

1. Name verifiable specifics
2. Delete summarizing endings
3. Cut meta-commentary on your own argument

Dual provenance does **not** promote extrapolation to measurement; weak rows gain nothing from it.
